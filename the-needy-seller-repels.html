Diary of a CCO · No. 03

The needy seller repels

September 2026 · 1 min read

Robert Greene has a law in The Laws of Human Nature that every salesperson learns the hard way. He calls it covetousness. We want what feels scarce, and what pulls away from us.

Push a deal and it backs up. The follow-ups pile on, the tone gets warmer, the discount comes out early, and the buyer feels all of it. Desperation has a smell, and it closes doors.

The needy seller repels. The seller who can walk away draws the room.

I have felt it from both sides. The vendor who needed my yes too much, I never called back. The deals I won came from the opposite place: I believed the prospect needed us more than we needed them, and I was ready to walk.

That readiness is not something you fake for one call. It comes from a full pipeline and from believing in what you sell. Invented scarcity is manipulation, and people feel that too. Real scarcity, you have options, you qualify hard, you do not chase, pulls people toward you.

Same product, opposite outcome.
