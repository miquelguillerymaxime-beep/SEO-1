Diary of a CCO · No. 02

Say no faster

September 2026 · 1 min read

More pipeline is the wrong goal.

Most founders want more leads. What they need is to say no faster.

A bad deal does not only cost you the deal. It costs you the three good ones you ignored while you kept it alive. The prospect who never had budget. The champion with no power. The "send me a deck" that was a polite exit.

Early on, a full pipeline felt like progress. It was noise wearing a suit.

Real qualifying is clearing the funnel of everything that will never close, so your week goes to the few deals that will.

Saying no is a skill nobody brags about. It looks like doing less. It is how you close more.
