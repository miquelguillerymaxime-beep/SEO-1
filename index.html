02 · The thesis

Most companies don't stall on a weak product. They stall because no one built the bridge to the people who need it.

The trap

A product too technical to evaluate, or a commercial function that never got built. Both leave a strong product with no path to its market.

The work

Build the bridge. The product is rarely the blocker. The connection to the buyer is, and that is what I do.
